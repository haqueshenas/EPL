## 1. What Is EPL?

The **Easy-Phenotyping Lab (EPL)** is a non-profit scientific initiative—just getting under way—to develop and share simple, novel, and reliable solutions for the widespread phenotyping challenges researchers in crop and plant sciences face globally. Building on the founder’s published high-throughput phenotyping (HTP) research, EPL develops and shares low-cost software tools, code snippets, computational models, and novel measurement approaches designed to make plant and crop phenotyping accessible to any lab, anywhere.

EPL also seeks to encourage a broader culture of open sharing in crop and plant phenotyping, so that useful tools and practical innovations can reach more researchers, particularly those who may not have access to specialized technical resources.

## 2. Open Crop Phenotyping (OCP)

**Open Crop Phenotyping (OCP)** was initiated by the **Easy-Phenotyping Lab (EPL)** and is offered as an open, shared commitment that no single laboratory owns. It invites researchers and developers of crop and plant phenotyping tools to share their software, methods, and practical innovations openly and freely, in forms that others can access, use, learn from, reproduce, and build upon.

OCP is guided by six shared principles:

1. **Reliability** – Scientifically sound methods, with code and procedures tested as carefully as reasonably possible.
2. **Simplicity** – Clear documentation, practical examples, and no unnecessary technical complexity.
3. **Availability** – Tools should be openly accessible and usable by researchers without unnecessary institutional or geographic barriers.
4. **Affordability** – The approach should avoid unnecessary dependence on expensive proprietary hardware or software.
5. **Reproducibility** – Documentation, sample data, parameters, or other information should be provided whenever practical so that results can be examined and reproduced.
6. **Community** – Researchers everywhere are welcome to use, test, discuss, improve, adapt, and share phenotyping tools.

Alongside **High-Throughput Phenotyping (HTP)**, OCP is also a step toward what may be called **High-Accessibility Crop Phenotyping**—making capable and trustworthy phenotyping tools available to more researchers, in more places, and with fewer barriers.

The complete OCP Commitment, including its purpose, principles, responsibility statement, and standard declarations for reuse, is maintained in the canonical document:

**[Open Crop Phenotyping (OCP) — OCP.md](OCP.md)**

OCP is a voluntary scientific commitment, not a software license, certification, or endorsement. Each developer or research group remains fully responsible for the scientific validity, technical performance, maintenance, documentation, and licensing of its own work.
